PERFORMANCE IMPACT
================================================================================

compute_dynamic_roadmap():
  - Q1 (courses): ~0.1–1.0 ms
  - Q2 (satisfied): ~0.1–2.0 ms (union_all of 3 subqueries)
  - Q3 (prerequisites): ~0.1–1.0 ms
  - Q4 (profiles): ~0.1–1.0 ms
  - Resolution: <0.5 ms (in-memory)
  - Total: ~1–5 ms for typical roadmaps (100–300 courses)

Compared to old /status endpoint:
  - Old: 1 query + N profile get-or-creates = O(N)
  - New: 4 fixed queries = O(1) DB cost
